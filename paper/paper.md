# Summary

**OpenLSR-X** is an open-source Python package built on PyTorch [@paszke2019pytorch] and PyTorch Lightning [@falcon2019lightning] for super-resolving 3D X-ray computed tomography (XCT) scans of porous media using 2D and `2.5D` neural architectures—shifted-window Transformers (`SwinIR` [@liang2021swinir]), Residual-in-Residual Dense Blocks (`RRDB` [@wang2018esrgan]) with relativistic adversarial training (`RaGAN` [@jolicoeur2019relativistic]), and residual SRGANs [@ledig2017photo]—paired with cell-centered 3D orthogonal Fourier fusion and a volumetric 3D CNN baseline (`SR3DResNet`). Alongside training and inference pipelines for synthetic porous media [@gostick2019porespy], multi-resolution rock micro-CT scans [@jackson2021bentheimer; @jackson2022multires], and synchrotron NeXus/HDF5 reconstructions [@green2025multimodal; @green2024zn13x_i13; @green2024zn13x_preview], OpenLSR-X provides an evaluation suite measuring image fidelity, 3D Fourier Shell Correlation (FSC), petrophysical properties (porosity, surface area, Euler connectivity, pore-radius distributions, and two-point correlations), and finite-volume 3D diffusive tortuosity ($D_{\text{eff}}/D_0$, $\tau$) with export to `OpenImpala` [@lehoux2021openimpala].

# Statement of Need

Three-dimensional micro-CT is foundational across hydrogeology, carbon storage, battery electrodes, catalysis, and rock mechanics [@blunt2013pore; @green2025multimodal]. However, XCT instruments enforce a trade-off between field of view (FOV) and voxel resolution: scans spanning a representative elementary volume (REV) frequently under-resolve sub-micron pore throats, biasing transport simulations [@wang2019deep].

While 3D super-resolution networks recover fine-scale pore geometry [@janssens2020computed], training 3D models on gigavoxel tomograms demands substantial GPU memory and registered 3D scan pairs that are costly to acquire [@green2025multimodal]. Conversely, applying a 2D network along one axis introduces through-plane stacking artifacts and spectral anisotropy. General-purpose vision toolboxes also evaluate super-resolution primarily via PSNR and SSIM, which correlate poorly with pore-throat connectivity and diffusive transport.

OpenLSR-X addresses these needs through:

1. **Memory-efficient 2D/`2.5D`-to-3D super-resolution** trained on spatially disjoint 3D slabs and reconstructed via cell-centered directional 3D Fourier and outlier-suppressed Fourier fusion across orthogonal (`yz`, `xz`, `xy`) planes, reporting inter-view disagreement $\sigma_{\text{ortho}}(\mathbf{x})$ (\autoref{fig:overview}).
2. **Split-isolated 3D benchmark infrastructure** that partitions 3D slabs before train-fitted intensity normalization, optional train-only cross-scan calibration (`affine` or monotonic `quantile`, persisted in checkpoints), and synthetic point-spread-function (PSF) degradation, preventing cross-plane voxel intersection and data leakage.
3. **Integrated petrophysical and transport diagnostics** evaluating reference-thresholded porosity, interfacial area, topology, 3D FSC, capillary drainage, and residual-verified steady-state 3D Fickian tortuosity against ground truth and 3D interpolation baselines.

# Comparison with Existing Open-Source Software

Adjacent open-source packages address parts of the workflow, but none combine split-isolated 2D/`2.5D`-to-3D tomographic super-resolution with petrophysical and diffusive transport validation:

- **2D Restoration Toolboxes (`BasicSR` [@wang2022basicsr]):** Provide 2D RGB super-resolution backbones, but lack cell-centered 3D orthogonal Fourier fusion, PSF-guarded 3D slab splitting, 32-bit BigTIFF and synchrotron NeXus/HDF5 loaders, and porous-media metrics.
- **3D Biomedical Frameworks (`MONAI` [@cardoso2022monai], `TorchIO` [@perez2021torchio]):** Excel at 3D medical patch sampling (`NIfTI`/`DICOM`) and segmentation, yet omit multi-axis 2D/`2.5D`-to-3D Fourier fusion and petrophysical diagnostics.
- **Porous-Media Suites (`PoreSpy` [@gostick2019porespy], `OpenPNM` [@gostick2016openpnm]):** Provide microstructure generators, morphological filters, and pore-network solvers, but lack learnable super-resolution models. OpenLSR-X complements `PoreSpy`, `OpenPNM`, and `OpenImpala` [@lehoux2021openimpala] upstream of pore-scale transport simulation.

# Architecture and Orthogonal 3D Fusion

OpenLSR-X provides modular generators (`SwinIRGenerator` [@liang2021swinir], `RRDBGenerator` [@wang2018esrgan], `Generator` [@ledig2017photo], and `SR3DResNet`) with native $2^a 3^b$ sub-pixel upsampling (`2x`, `3x`, `4x`, `6x`, `8x`) and arbitrary fractional-scale heads (`2.5x`, `5x`) for single-channel XCT attenuation data:

- **Boundary-safe padding & normalization:** Half-sample symmetric spatial padding eliminates boundary phase shifts and rim artifacts, while feature normalization (`PixelNorm` [@karras2018progressive]) or normalization-free trunks (`RRDB`/`SwinIR`) preserve greyscale attenuation scales at small batch sizes.
- **Domain-specific objectives:** A unified `SRGAN` `LightningModule` supports $L_1$/MSE training, generator warmup prior to standard or relativistic (`RaGAN` [@jolicoeur2019relativistic]) adversarial training, approximate slice/`2.5D` attenuation consistency (`AttenuationConservationLoss`), soft-skeleton connectivity (`SoftCLDiceLoss` [@shit2021cldice]), 2D/3D Sobel sharpness, Focal Frequency Loss [@jiang2021focal], and VGG-19 perceptual supervision.
- **Cell-Centered 3D Orthogonal Fourier Fusion:** For $V_{\text{LR}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times h \times w}$ and scale $s$, OpenLSR-X predicts cell-centered sweeps $\hat{V}_{yz}, \hat{V}_{xz}, \hat{V}_{xy} \in \mathbb{R}^{sd \times sh \times sw}$ (`scipy.ndimage.zoom(..., grid_mode=True)`) from native-LR-strided `2.5D` context and fuses their boundary-padded 3D Fourier transforms (`rfftn`) weighted by in-plane spatial wavevectors, alongside voxel-wise $\sigma_{\text{ortho}}(\mathbf{x})$ ($n=3$).

# Petrophysical Validation and Reproducible Benchmarks

OpenLSR-X ships with analytical verification tests (`tests/test_analytical_verification.py`, `scripts/verification/run_verification_suite.py`), provenance-tracked benchmarks (`scripts/benchmarks/`), and beamline configs (`configs/`):

1. **Closed-Form Analytical Verification:** Validates cell-centered alignment ($\le 2.3 \times 10^{-5}$ HR voxels across $2\times$–$8\times$), integer HR-grid 3D registration, split-isolated cross-scan calibration, steady-state 3D Fickian diffusion ($0.0001\%$–$0.80\%$ error across open space, straight ducts, dead-end cavities, and Maxwell spheres), optical MTF recovery ($\le 0.26\%$ error), 3D Euler characteristic ($\chi = +1$ ball, $\chi = 0$ torus), and non-periodic two-point correlation ($\text{RMSE} = 0.0003$).
2. **Factorial PoreSpy Study:** Benchmarks single-scale and dual-porosity 3D synthetic media [@gostick2019porespy] across blur and noise regimes against cell-centered 3D interpolation, `SRResNet`, single-axis `OpenLSR-X`, and 3-axis Fourier-fused `OpenLSR-X`.
3. **Open Rock Micro-CT & Synchrotron Profiles:** Evaluates Bentheimer Sandstone micro-CT stacks [@jackson2021bentheimer; @jackson2022multires]—under synthetic $2\times$ degradation and physical $18\ \mu\text{m} \to 6\ \mu\text{m}$ ($3\times$) dual-scan pairs—plus calibrated carbonate phantoms and coaxial sub-voxel-registered pairs from the Diamond Light Source I13-2 multi-resolution `Zn-13X` dataset [@green2025multimodal; @green2024zn13x_i13; @green2024zn13x_preview], recording explicit provenance (`source_kind`) and exporting `booktabs` LaTeX tables and `OpenImpala` [@lehoux2021openimpala] decks.
