# Summary

**OpenLSR-X** is an open-source Python package built on PyTorch [@paszke2019pytorch] and PyTorch Lightning [@falcon2019lightning] for super-resolving three-dimensional X-ray computed tomography (XCT) scans of porous materials using two-dimensional and `2.5D` neural architectures—including shifted-window Transformers (`SwinIR`), Residual-in-Residual Dense Blocks (`RRDB`/`RaGAN`), and residual SRGANs [@ledig2017photo]—combined with cell-centered three-dimensional orthogonal Fourier fusion and a volumetric 3D CNN baseline (`SR3DResNet`). Alongside training and inference pipelines supporting synthetic porous media [@gostick2019porespy], laboratory micro-CT stacks, and multi-resolution synchrotron NeXus/HDF5 reconstructions [@green2025multimodal; @green2024zn13x_i13], OpenLSR-X provides a quantitative evaluation suite measuring classical image fidelity, 3D Fourier Shell Correlation (FSC) agreement, segmented petrophysical properties (porosity, specific surface area, Euler connectivity, inscribed pore-radius distributions, and two-point spatial correlations), and cell-centered finite-volume 3D diffusive tortuosity ($D_{\text{eff}}/D_0$, $\tau$) with direct export to `OpenImpala`.

# Statement of Need

Three-dimensional X-ray micro-CT is a foundational characterization technique across hydrogeology, carbon capture and storage, battery electrodes, catalysis, and rock mechanics [@blunt2013pore; @green2025multimodal]. However, XCT instruments enforce an inherent trade-off between sample field of view (FOV) and voxel resolution: scans broad enough to capture a representative elementary volume (REV) frequently under-resolve sub-micron pore throats, biasing multiphase flow and transport simulations [@wang2019deep].

While 3D convolutional and generative super-resolution models can recover fine-scale pore geometry [@janssens2020computed], training fully 3D networks on gigavoxel tomograms requires substantial GPU memory and registered 3D low/high-resolution scan pairs that are expensive to acquire and align [@green2025multimodal]. Conversely, applying a 2D slice-wise super-resolution network along a single tomographic axis introduces through-plane discontinuities ("venetian blind" stacking artifacts) and anisotropic frequency distortion. Furthermore, general-purpose computer-vision toolboxes evaluate super-resolution almost exclusively via PSNR and SSIM, which correlate poorly with topological pore-throat preservation and diffusive transport.

OpenLSR-X addresses these needs by providing porous-media and beamline researchers with:

1. **Memory-efficient 2D/`2.5D`-to-3D super-resolution** that trains on slices from spatially disjoint 3D slabs and reconstructs isotropic 3D volumes via cell-centered directional 3D Fourier and outlier-suppressed Fourier fusion across orthogonal (`yz`, `xz`, `xy`) planes, while reporting inter-view disagreement $\sigma_{\text{ortho}}(\mathbf{x})$ across the three sweeps (\autoref{fig:overview}).
2. **Split-isolated 3D benchmark infrastructure** that partitions 3D slabs prior to train-fitted intensity normalization, optional train-only cross-scan radiometric calibration (`affine` or monotonic `quantile`), and synthetic point-spread-function (PSF) degradation, preventing cross-plane voxel intersection and intensity leakage across independent multi-resolution scans.
3. **Integrated petrophysical and pore-scale transport diagnostics** that evaluate reference-thresholded porosity, interfacial area, topology, 3D FSC spectral agreement, capillary drainage curves, and residual-verified steady-state 3D Fickian diffusive tortuosity relative to ground truth and 3D interpolation baselines.

# Comparison with Existing Open-Source Software

Several mature open-source packages address adjacent parts of the imaging or porous-media workflow, but none integrate split-isolated 2D/`2.5D`-to-3D tomographic super-resolution with quantitative petrophysical and diffusive transport validation:

- **2D Natural-Image Restoration Toolboxes (`BasicSR` [@wang2022basicsr]):** Provide broad 2D RGB super-resolution backbones, but lack cell-centered 3D orthogonal Fourier volume fusion, spatially guarded 3D slab splitting, scientific 32-bit BigTIFF and synchrotron NeXus/HDF5 ROI loaders, and porous-media transport metrics.
- **3D Biomedical Deep-Learning Frameworks (`MONAI` [@cardoso2022monai], `TorchIO` [@perez2021torchio]):** Excel at 3D medical patch sampling (`NIfTI`/`DICOM`) and volumetric segmentation, yet do not implement multi-axis 2D/`2.5D`-to-3D frequency-domain fusion for memory-constrained gigavoxel micro-CT or topological/petrophysical microstructure diagnostics.
- **Porous-Media Characterization Suites (`PoreSpy` [@gostick2019porespy], `OpenPNM` [@gostick2016openpnm]):** Provide synthetic microstructure generators, morphological filters, and pore-network transport solvers, but do not include learnable deep super-resolution models. OpenLSR-X directly complements `PoreSpy`, `OpenPNM`, and `OpenImpala` as an upstream super-resolution and fidelity-benchmarking framework prior to pore-network or direct numerical transport simulation.

# Architecture and Orthogonal 3D Fusion

OpenLSR-X provides modular generator backbones (`SwinIRGenerator`, `RRDBGenerator`, `Generator`, and `SR3DResNet`) with native $2^a 3^b$ sub-pixel upsampling (`2x`, `3x`, `4x`, `6x`, `8x`) plus arbitrary fractional-scale heads (`2.5x`, `5x`), alongside domain-specific objectives tailored for single-channel quantitative XCT attenuation data:

- **Boundary-safe convolutions & normalization:** Reflection/replicate padding eliminates zero-padding rim artifacts across cropped sub-slices, while channel-wise feature vector normalization (`PixelNorm`) [@karras2018progressive] or normalization-free residual trunks (`RRDB`/`SwinIR`) preserve greyscale attenuation scales under small batch sizes.
- **Domain-specific loss functions:** A unified `SRGAN` `LightningModule` supports generator-only $L_1$/MSE training, scheduled generator warmup prior to standard or relativistic (`RaGAN`) adversarial activation, approximate slice/`2.5D` linear-attenuation downsampling consistency (`AttenuationConservationLoss`), soft-skeleton connectivity (`SoftCLDiceLoss`), Sobel gradient sharpness, Focal Frequency Loss, and optional VGG-19 perceptual supervision.
- **Cell-Centered 3D Orthogonal Fourier Fusion:** Given a low-resolution 3D scan $V_{\text{LR}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times h \times w}$ and scale factor $s$, OpenLSR-X generates three cell-centered candidate reconstructions $\hat{V}_{yz}, \hat{V}_{xz}, \hat{V}_{xy} \in \mathbb{R}^{sd \times sh \times sw}$ (`scipy.ndimage.zoom(..., grid_mode=True)`) using native-LR-strided `2.5D` slice context and fuses their 3D discrete Fourier transforms (`rfftn`) weighted by each view's super-resolved in-plane spatial wavevectors, while computing the voxel-wise inter-view standard deviation $\sigma_{\text{ortho}}(\mathbf{x})$ ($n=3$).

# Petrophysical Validation and Reproducible Benchmarks

To support reproducible methods comparisons across synthetic, laboratory, and synchrotron regimes, OpenLSR-X ships with an automated closed-form analytical verification suite (`tests/test_analytical_verification.py`, `scripts/verification/run_verification_suite.py`), provenance-tracked benchmark suites (`scripts/benchmarks/`), and beamline configuration profiles (`configs/`):

1. **Closed-Form Analytical Verification:** Validates cell-centered coordinate alignment ($\le 2.3 \times 10^{-5}$ HR voxels across $2\times$, $3\times$, $4\times$, and $8\times$), half-LR-voxel 3D registration, split-isolated cross-scan radiometric calibration, steady-state 3D Fickian diffusion ($0.0001\%$–$0.80\%$ error against open bulk space, parallel straight ducts, zero-flux dead-end cavities, and Maxwell sphere suspensions), effective optical MTF recovery ($\le 0.26\%$ vs. exact Gaussian transfer functions), 3D Euler characteristic ($\chi = +1$ ball, $\chi = 0$ torus), and non-periodic two-point correlation ($\text{RMSE} = 0.0003$).
2. **Factorial PoreSpy Study:** Evaluates single-scale and dual-porosity 3D synthetic media generated with PoreSpy [@gostick2019porespy] across controlled detector blur and noise regimes, comparing cell-centered 3D Nearest, Trilinear, and Tricubic B-spline interpolation against `SRResNet`, single-axis `OpenLSR-X`, and 3-axis Fourier-fused `OpenLSR-X`.
3. **Open Rock Micro-CT & Synchrotron Transport Profiles:** Evaluates super-resolution on Bentheimer Sandstone micro-CT stacks—under both controlled synthetic $2\times$ degradation and physical independent $18\ \mu\text{m} \to 6\ \mu\text{m}$ ($3\times$) dual-scan pairs (`10.5281/zenodo.5542624`)—alongside calibrated carbonate phantoms and coaxial sub-voxel-registered pairs from the open Diamond Light Source I13-2 multi-resolution zinc-doped Zeolite 13X dataset [@green2025multimodal; @green2024zn13x_i13], recording explicit dataset provenance (`source_kind`) in every output table and exporting publication-ready `booktabs` LaTeX tables and `OpenImpala` simulation decks.
